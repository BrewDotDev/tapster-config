# Tapster Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 11, 2026_

Tapster is a casual bar game for iPhone. We built it to collect as little as possible.

## What we collect

**Nothing about you.** Tapster has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We don't collect your name, email, location, contacts or device identifiers, and we don't share or sell any data.

## What stays on your device

Your progress, unlocked guests, tips, settings and look are saved only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them.

## Apple services

- **Game Center** (optional): if you're signed in, your scores are sent to Apple's Game Center leaderboards. Apple handles this under the [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
- **In-app purchases**: payments are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details.

## Network requests

The app downloads one public file, the Daily Pour rules, from GitHub. The request contains no personal information. GitHub may log standard request data such as IP addresses under the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Notifications

If you turn on Daily reminders, they are scheduled locally on your iPhone. No notification data leaves your device.

## Children

Tapster doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
